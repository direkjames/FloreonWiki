## **Uses 2 Pedestals at the Throneroom of Knightly Heroes**

There's no detailed instruction on how to summon these two legendaries but based on our research that you can summon them in the Throneroom of Knightly Heroes. Use your Arc Phone to find the structure.

To summon Zacian, place the Hero Sword in the first pedestal and put a Totem of Undying in the second pedestal.

To summon Zamazenta, place the Hero Shield in the first pedestal and put a Totem of Undying in the second pedestal.
