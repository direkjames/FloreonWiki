Terapagos is summoned at the **Crystal Cavern**. Use a waystone to travel there: the waystone is called **Crystal Cavern**.

## What you need

- **1 Terastal Core**
- **50 Stellar Tera Shards**
- No weather is needed. You can do it at any time.

Stand at the altar in the middle of the build and right-click it. If something is missing, the altar tells you what. When everything is right, the altar glows for a few seconds and then Terapagos appears.

## Getting the keys

- **Terastal Core:** beat **Geeta**, the Paldea Champion, in the Paldea League. She gives a Terastal Core the first time you win. You can also buy it in the **BP Shop** at the Battle Tower for **200 BP**.
- **Stellar Tera Shards:** these are items from the Mega Showdown mod. You need 50 of them.

## Good to know

- Terapagos appears at **level 70**, with a 2% chance of being shiny.
- For **300 seconds** (5 minutes) only you can battle it or catch it. Other players get a message if they try.
- If nobody beats it, Terapagos disappears after **10 minutes**.
- After you summon Terapagos, you must wait **3 days** before you can use this altar again.
- The Terastal Core and all 50 Stellar Tera Shards are used up each time.
