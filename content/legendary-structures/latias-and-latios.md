## **Uses a Pedestal at the Southern Island**

To obtain Latias and Latios you need to craft a "Latias Treat" and "Latios Treat", for which you will require multiple cobblemon berries and a "**Poketreat Box**" which the **Entrepreneur Villager** sometimes sells.

Once you have the treat, you need to find the **Southern Island** (you can track it using the **Arc Phone**) and place the treat at the **Lati Pedestal** and a level 50 Latias or Latios will spawn, with a 2% chance of it being a shiny.

Keep in mind that Latias and Latios share their pedestal, and you must choose between one or the other, and to **obtain the other you will have to find another island**.
