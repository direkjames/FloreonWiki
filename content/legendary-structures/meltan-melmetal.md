## **Uses the Meltan Box**

To obtain Meltan you must craft a "**Meltan Box**" and place it on the floor, after that, you need to fill the box with a total of 50 Metal Value, with the possible items being:

* Copper Ingot -> 1 point
* Iron Ingot -> 2 points
* Gold Ingot -> 3 points
* Netherite Ingot -> 50 points

After that, just right-click the box and a level 5 Meltan will spawn, with a 2% chance of it being a shiny (The Box won't be consumed), from then on, that specific box will start giving you 1 "**Meltan Candy**" for every 50 points of Metal Value, and by giving 64 candies to Meltan, you will be able to evolve him into **Melmetal**.
