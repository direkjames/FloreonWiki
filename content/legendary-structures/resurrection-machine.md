Some Pokémon were made by people in the games, not born in the wild. On Floreon you can make them yourself in the **Fossil Resurrection Machine** (the monitor and tank from Cobblemon).

> [!NOTE] The only way
> Mewtwo, Genesect, Magearna and Type: Null do **not** spawn in the wild on Floreon. The Resurrection Machine is the only way to get them. Silvally comes only from evolving a Type: Null.

## How to use it

1. Put the **two items** for the Pokémon you want into the machine's monitor.
2. Let the machine finish its process.
3. The Pokémon is revived at **level 1** with random IVs.

> [!NOTE] Level 1
> You get a level 1 Pokémon. You will need to train it. Your level cap in the [Pokémon League](../pokemon-league/index.md) still applies.

## Recipes

| Pokémon | Put in the monitor | Lore |
|---|---|---|
| Mewtwo | Tuft of Mew Hair + Nether Star | Cloned from Mew's genes |
| Genesect | Old Amber Fossil + Metal Coat | An ancient Bug and Steel Pokémon, revived and fitted with Steel parts |
| Magearna | Steel Gem + Fairy Gem | An artificial Steel and Fairy Pokémon |
| Type: Null | Dubious Disc + Metal Coat | A synthetic chimera made by combining many Pokémon |

The **Tuft of Mew Hair** comes from the Mew ritual, see [Mew](mew.md). The **Nether Star** drops from the Wither.

## Silvally

Silvally can't be made directly. Make a **Type: Null**, then raise its **friendship to 160** and level it up. It evolves into Silvally. Use Memory discs from Mega Showdown to change Silvally's type.

> [!TIP] How to raise friendship
> Feed your Type: Null **Pomeg-type berries** (Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa, Tamato), give it **Poké Puffs** it likes, keep it in your party and level it up. A held **Soothe Bell** and a **Luxury Ball** make it grow faster. Healing items like Remedy lower it.
