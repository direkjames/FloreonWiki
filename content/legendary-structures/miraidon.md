Miraidon is summoned at the **Future Lab**. Use a waystone to travel there: the waystone is called **Future Lab**. The lab is in the **End**.

## What you need

- **1 Future Core**
- No weather is needed. You can do it at any time.

Stand at the altar in the middle of the build and right-click it. If something is missing, the altar tells you what. When everything is right, the altar glows for a few seconds and then Miraidon appears.

## Getting the keys

- Beat **Iono** in the Paldea League. She gives a Future Core the first time you win.
- Buy it in the **BP Shop** at the Battle Tower for **200 BP**.

## Good to know

- Miraidon appears at **level 70**, with a 2% chance of being shiny.
- For **300 seconds** (5 minutes) only you can battle it or catch it. Other players get a message if they try.
- If nobody beats it, Miraidon disappears after **10 minutes**.
- After you summon Miraidon, you must wait **3 days** before you can use this altar again.
- The Future Core is used up each time.
