Kyogre is summoned at the **Marine Shrine**. Use a waystone to travel there: the waystone is called **Marine Shrine**.

## What you need

- **1 Aqua Core**
- **Rain**

Stand at the altar in the middle of the build and right-click it. If something is missing, the altar tells you what. When everything is right, the altar glows for a few seconds and then Kyogre appears.

## Getting an Aqua Core

- Beat **Wallace** in the Hoenn League. He gives an Aqua Core the first time you win.
- Buy it in the **BP Shop** at the Battle Tower for **200 BP**.

## Good to know

- Kyogre appears at **level 70**, with a 2% chance of being shiny.
- For **300 seconds** (5 minutes) only you can battle it or catch it. Other players get a message if they try.
- If nobody beats it, Kyogre disappears after **10 minutes**.
- After you summon Kyogre, you must wait **3 days** before you can use this altar again.
- The Aqua Core is used up each time.
