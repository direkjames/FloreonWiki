![Articuno: screenshot 1](/images/articuno-1.webp)

To obtain Articuno you need to craft a "**Urn of Frost**" and defeat 50 ice-type pokemon, either by using melee or through pokemon battle, whilst you have the urn in your inventory. (You can't fill multiple urns at the same time).

Once the urn is filled all you need to do is right-click with it in your hand and a level 40 Articuno will spawn, with a 2% chance of it being a shiny.

You will also receive a "**Arctic Stone**" upon using the filled urn, which you'll be able to put together with the other 2 bird stones to craft the "Vortex Stone" to obtain Lugia .

![Articuno: screenshot 2](/images/articuno-2.webp)

To obtain the Galarian Form simply use a "**Galarian Urn**" instead
