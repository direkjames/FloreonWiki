---
title: Legendary Structures
description: How to summon the legendary Pokémon on Floreon.
---
Many legendary Pokémon on Floreon are summoned through a structure, an urn or a ritual. Pick a legendary from the list on the left, or use the search.

Some Pokémon made by people in the games, such as Mewtwo, Genesect, Magearna, Type: Null and Silvally, are made in the Resurrection Machine instead. See [Cloning with the Resurrection Machine](resurrection-machine.md).

Legendaries that have no structure yet only appear in the wild when their conditions are met. Those are listed on [No Structures Yet](no-structures-yet.md).
