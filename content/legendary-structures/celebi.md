## **Uses the Illex Shrine Block**

To obtain Celebi you must first craft a **GS Ball** using "**Yellow Apricorns**" and a "**Netherite Scrap**"

### GS Ball Crafting Recipe

After that you must find the Ilex Shrine which spawns naturally in the Spooky Biomes.
