Rayquaza is summoned at the **Sky Pillar**. Use a waystone to travel there: the waystone is called **Sky Pillar**. Then climb the ladder to the top.

## What you need

- **1 Magma Core**, **1 Aqua Core** and **1 Sky Meteorite**, all at once
- A **thunderstorm**

Stand at the altar on top of the pillar and right-click it. If something is missing, the altar tells you what. When everything is right, the altar glows for a few seconds and then Rayquaza appears.

## Getting the keys

| Key | Beat this trainer in the League (first win only) | Or buy it in the BP Shop |
|---|---|---|
| Magma Core | Flannery (Hoenn) | 200 BP |
| Aqua Core | Wallace (Hoenn) | 200 BP |
| Sky Meteorite | Steven Stone (Hoenn Champion) | 200 BP |

The BP Shop is at the Battle Tower.

## Good to know

- Rayquaza appears at **level 70**, with a 2% chance of being shiny.
- For **300 seconds** (5 minutes) only you can battle it or catch it. Other players get a message if they try.
- If nobody beats it, Rayquaza disappears after **10 minutes**.
- After you summon Rayquaza, you must wait **3 days** before you can use this altar again.
- All three keys are used up each time.
