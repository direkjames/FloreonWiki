The Battle Tower is a challenge where you climb floor after floor of battles. Every floor you win earns you **BP** (Battle Points), which you spend in the **BP Shop**.

## Who can enter

| Mode | You need to |
|---|---|
| Battle Tower | Defeat the **Unova Champion** in the [Pokémon League](../pokemon-league/index.md) |
| Boss Mode | Defeat **all the Pokémon Champions**, which means finishing the whole League |

## Earning BP

You earn BP every time you **win a floor**. Higher floors give more:

| Floors | BP per win |
|---|---|
| 1 to 10 | 2 |
| 11 to 25 | 3 |
| 26 to 50 | 4 |
| 51 to 100 | 5 |
| 101 to 200 | 6 |
| 201 to 999 | 8 |

> [!NOTE] Weekly cap
> You can earn at most **250 BP per week**.

## Spending BP

Spend your BP in the **BP Shop**.
