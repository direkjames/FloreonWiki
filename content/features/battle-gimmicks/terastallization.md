What is 'Terastallization'?

Terastallization is a battle gimmick from Pokémon. It allows the user to switch the current pokemon type via toggle. It is also able to boost the certain type power aside from STAB types.

**For example:** A Torchic can terastallize to a fighting type. Replacing the fire type for the entire battle. Their weakness and resistance will be changed depending on the Terastal type.

How to Terastallize

Craft and equip a **Tera Orb** or alternatively an **Omni Ring**.

How to change a Pokémon's Tera Type

Hold **50 of a specific Tera Shard** and right click your Pokémon.

There are tera pouches to store the shards.
