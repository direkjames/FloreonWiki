What is 'Dynamax'?

Dynamax is a battle mechanic causing a Pokémon to grow massively in size for 3 turns, gaining a boost to their HP, & having their moves turned into Max Moves, each Type having their own effect that can turn the tides of the battle.

Certain Pokémon will be able to Gigantamax instead changing into a whole new form, with their own signature G-Max Move of a matching Type, & unique effects!

## How it Works

The boost in their HP is determined by their Dynamax Level. Starting with 50%, each level increased is a 5% boost, totaling up to an additional 100% HP! To increase the level, you'll need to feed your Pokémon Dynamax Candy.

How to Dynamax a Pokémon

First, you need a **Dynamax Band** in your Accessories slot. You are also required to be near a **Power Spot** within **32 blocks**.

## In Battle

![Clicking the button and playing the move will Dynamax the Pokémon.](/images/dynamax-2.webp)

Entering battle, you will find a button to Dynamax your Pokémon in the move selection screen, next to the back button. You can not Dynamax outside battle.

Exceptions

**Zacian**, **Zamazenta**, & **Eternatus** can not Dynamax, but they each have their own respective transformations.
