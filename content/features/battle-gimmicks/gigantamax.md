What is 'Gigantamax'?

Gigantamax is a form of Dynamax for certain species of Pokémon that have the Gigantamax Factor, transforming into a new, even bigger, form of that Pokémon, gaining their own special G-Max Move with a unique effect only it can use!

How to Gigantamax a Pokémon

Those of which are part of this list of implemented Gigantamax in the mainline series. You'll need a Pokémon with the Gigantamax Factor, if it does not have that already to allow a Pokemon to Gigantamax, you need to feed it **Max Soup**.

### In Battle

Gigantamax is performed the same way you would Dynamax your Pokémon, you can refer back to it.

### Urshifu

Unlike the other Gigantamax Pokémon, instead of Max Soup, Urshifu will require the Sweet Max Soup.
