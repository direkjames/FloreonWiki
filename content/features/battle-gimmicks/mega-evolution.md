> [!NOTE] Pokémon League rule
> In Pokémon League battles, Mega Evolution unlocks in the Hoenn League. See [Gimmick rules](../../pokemon-league/gimmick-rules.md).

![Mega Evolution: screenshot 1](/images/mega-evolution-1.webp)

Mega Evolution is a battle mechanic in which a Pokémon takes on a different form for the rest of a battle, greatly enhancing the Pokémon's power!

## How to Mega Evolve a Pokémon

First you need to obtain the respective **Mega Stone** using a **Raw Mega Stone** found in **Megasites**. You also need a **Mega Ring**, which can be crafted with a **Key Stone**.

### In Battle

In order to Mega Evolve during a Pokémon battle, you must click on the mega evolve button below the Move list and then select a Move.

![Clicking the button and playing the move will mega evolve the Pokémon.](/images/mega-evolution-2.webp)

### Outside Battle

If you are wearing any Mega Accessory & the Pokémon has its respective Mega Stone equipped - **Sneak + Right-Click** on the Pokémon and click on the Mega Icon.

![Mega Evolution: screenshot 3](/images/mega-evolution-3.webp)

### Mega Rayquaza

Mega Rayquaza is unique from other Mega Evolution in that it does not require a Mega Stone to Mega Evolve.

Instead, you must teach it **Dragon Ascent**.
