What are 'Z-Moves'?

Z-Moves are a battle mechanic that allow a Pokémon to unleash a very powerful Move!

## How it Works

If a Pikachu is holding a Grass-Type Z-Crystal, It can power up any Grass-Type Move Pikachu has with a big boost in damage, stronger than your average Grass-Type move when used! However it can only be triggered once per battle.

How to use a Z-Move

A **Z-Ring** is required to be equipped in order to perform Z-Moves, also a **Z-Crystal** is also required to be held by a Pokémon in order to perform it

## In Battle

A button will appear below your Moves, click it & the matching Moves of your Z-Crystal with become Z-Moves, click a Z-Move & your Pokémon will deliver a powerful Move!
