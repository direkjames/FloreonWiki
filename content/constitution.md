Our community is built on the foundation of respect, fair play, and a shared love for a peaceful, stress-free Minecraft experience. To ensure that our server remains a welcoming, organized, and enjoyable home for everyone, we have established this official rulebook.

The articles outlined below are not just suggestions; they are the core laws that protect our players and govern our community. By logging in and playing on Floreon, you acknowledge and agree to abide by these rules in their entirety.

Please read through them carefully, ignorance of the rules is never accepted as a valid excuse for breaking them.

## **Article I: Chat, Content, & Communication**

Goal: Maintain an organized, appropriate, and on-topic environment.

* **1.1 Spamming & Flooding:** Sending repeated, meaningless, or excessively long messages, symbols, or characters that disrupt the flow of chat is strictly prohibited.  
  **Example:** Pasting a wall of text, pressing enter after every single word, or spamming "HELLO" five times in a row.
* **1.2 Channel Relevance:** All communication must be directed to its appropriate, designated channel.  
  **Example:** Posting memes in a serious announcements channel or asking for technical support in a general chat channel.
* **1.3 Prohibited Content (NSFW):** Any content, discussion, imagery, or links depicting sexually explicit material, extreme gore, or illegal acts are strictly forbidden.  
  **Example:** Linking to an adult website, using inappropriate ASCII art, or setting a profile picture that violates these standards.
* **1.4 Extremism & Harmful Ideologies:** Promoting, discussing, or joking about terrorist organizations, extremist hate groups, or harmful political ideologies is not allowed.  
  **Example:** Defending hate crimes or sharing symbols associated with hate groups.
* **1.5 Trolling & Disruption:** Deliberately attempting to anger other players, derail conversations, or cause chaos in chat or gameplay is prohibited.  
  **Example:** Purposefully giving new players false and dangerous information, like telling them to jump in lava for an achievement.
* **1.6 Public Arguments:** Disputes between players must not be aired in public channels. If a disagreement escalates, take it to private messages or open a support ticket.  
  **Example:** Cursing at another player in global chat over a stolen item instead of reporting it to staff.
* **1.7 Advertising & Poaching:** Promoting, discussing, or distributing IP addresses/links for unauthorized Minecraft or Discord servers is strictly forbidden.  
  **Example:** Saying "This server is boring, join my server at \[IP Address]" or direct messaging players to recruit them elsewhere.

## **Article II: Respect & Fair Play**

Goal: Ensure a safe, fair, and welcoming environment for all players.

* **2.1 Stealing, Scamming, & Griefing:** Taking items without permission, tricking players out of their belongings, or destroying player builds (griefing) is strictly banned.  
  **Example:** Renaming a piece of dirt to "Diamond Block" to trick a player in a trade, or breaking someone's chests while they are offline.
* **2.2 Bribery & Corruption:** Offering in-game items, real-world money, favors, or roles in exchange for rule immunity, unbans, or unfair advantages is prohibited.  
  **Example:** Offering a staff member $10 or PHP 600 to unban your friend.
* **2.3 Begging:** Excessively asking staff or players for free items, ranks, or money will result in mutes or kicks.  
  **Example:** Repeatedly messaging a wealthy player, "Pls give me diamonds, I'm poor, please please please."
* **2.4 Hate Speech & Offensive Language:** The use of racial, sexual, homophobic, or otherwise discriminatory slurs, remarks, or "jokes" carries a zero-tolerance policy.  
  **Example:** Using slurs in chat, even if censored with asterisks, or building offensive words out of blocks.
* **2.5 PvP Consent & Safe Zones:** Player-versus-Player combat is only allowed in designated areas (e.g., /warp PVP) or with explicit, mutual agreement in chat.  
  **Example:** Pushing an AFK player out of a safe zone to kill them, or attacking a builder who has not agreed to fight.
* **2.6 Fair & In-Game Trading (No IRL Trading):** All trades must be honest and contained entirely within the game. Trading in-game items for real-world money or out-of-game perks is banned.  
  **Example:** Trading a stack of enchanted apples for a Discord Nitro subscription.

## **Article III: Property & Building**

Goal: Protect player creations, maintain optimal server performance, and ensure fair use of game dimensions.

* **3.1 Trespassing & Unauthorized Building:** You may not enter private, claimed land, or build on/around another player's property without their explicit consent.  
  **Example:** Building a massive dirt pillar right outside someone's claim boundary to block their view.
* **3.2 Banned Player Property:** Areas owned by banned players remain protected until the end of the season. Do not loot or claim them without official staff approval.  
  **Example:** Finding out your neighbor was banned and immediately breaking into their base to steal their beacons.
* **3.3 Settlement Proximity:** You must communicate with nearby landowners before settling or building close to existing claimed areas. Give people space to expand.  
  **Example:** Claiming a chunk immediately adjacent to a growing town without asking the town's mayor first.
* **3.4 Claim Stealing & Unauthorized Claims:** Claiming another player's base, a server-built structure, or community builds is strictly forbidden. Staff will remove these claims.  
  **Example:** Finding an unclaimed house someone built yesterday and using a claiming tool to lock them out of it.
* **3.5 Server Performance & Lag:** Builds, redstone machines, or mob farms that cause excessive server lag or disrupt gameplay for others are not allowed and will be dismantled.  
  **Example:** Breeding 500 cows in a single pen or creating a fast, infinite redstone clock that never turns off.
* **3.6 The Nether Roof:** Accessing, building, or placing any blocks on top of the Nether roof (above the bedrock ceiling) is strictly prohibited. Any structures or portals found up there will be deleted.  
  **Example:** Using ender pearls or minecarts to glitch through the top bedrock layer of the Nether to build a highly efficient gold farm or a safe travel highway.
* **3.7 Artificial Chunk Loading & Stasis Chambers:** The creation or use of contraptions designed to artificially keep chunks loaded when a player is not physically present (such as nether portal chunk loaders) is strictly prohibited as it heavily degrades server performance. Consequently, long-distance ender pearl stasis chambers that rely on artificial chunk loading are also banned.  
  **Example:** Building a redstone system that continuously shoots items back and forth through a Nether portal to keep your automatic farms running while you are offline, or using a chunk-loaded stasis chamber to instantly teleport across the map from thousands of blocks away.

## **Article IV: Account, Privacy, & Safety**

Goal: Protect personal information and ensure individual accountability.

* **4.1 Unauthorized Account Access:** Never log into or use another player’s account, even if they give you permission. You are responsible for your own account.  
  **Example:** Logging into a sibling's account to bypass a temporary ban or transfer their money to yourself.
* **4.2 Privacy & Doxxing:** Sharing personal, identifying information about yourself or any other player without consent is strictly prohibited.  
  **Example:** Posting someone's real-life name, school, city, or social media links in chat.
* **4.3 Impersonation:** Pretending to be another player, a staff member, or a public figure using usernames, nicknames, or skins is forbidden.  
  **Example:** Changing your nickname to "Admin\_Steve" to trick players into giving you their passwords.
* **4.4 Alternate Accounts (Alts):** Using secondary accounts is not permitted. One player equals one account.  
  **Example:** Using a second account to load chunks for a farm while your main account is exploring.
* **4.5 Threats of Harm (Zero Tolerance):** Any threats of physical violence, death, swatting, doxxing, or self-harm will result in an immediate, permanent, and unappealable ban.  
  **Example:** Telling another player to "kill themselves" (even as an abbreviation like "kys") or threatening to find out where they live.

## **Article V: Cheating, Exploits, & Tools**

Goal: Maintain a level playing field, preserve the server economy, and ensure fair mechanics for the entire community.

* **5.1 Hacked Clients & Unfair Modifications:** The use of client-side modifications that provide an unfair advantage over vanilla players is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to, X-Ray, KillAura, Aimbot, Fly hacks, Speed hacks, or Freecam.  
  **Example:** Using a resource pack that makes stone transparent to easily mine diamonds, or using a client that automatically aims your sword and hits players faster than normally possible.
* **5.2 Macros & Automation:** The use of hardware macros, software auto-clickers, or physical alterations to automate gameplay is not allowed. You must be actively present at your screen to perform actions. "I was just testing it" or "My sibling was playing" are not valid excuses.  
  **Example:** Placing a heavy object on your mouse button to continuously mine a cobblestone generator, or setting up a keyboard macro to automatically sell items to the server shop while you are asleep.
* **5.3 Bug Abuse & Exploitation:** Deliberately utilizing Minecraft glitches, plugin errors, or server economy flaws for personal gain is banned. If you discover a bug, you must report it to staff via a ticket immediately, rather than testing or using it.  
  **Example:** Finding a glitch that allows you to duplicate shulker boxes and filling your chests with them, or discovering a server shop that accidentally buys an item for more money than it sells for and abusing it for infinite wealth.
* **5.4 Block Glitching & Boundary Exploits:** Using game mechanics or glitches to phase through walls, bypass barriers, enter restricted areas, or escape the designated world border is prohibited.  
  **Example:** Throwing an ender pearl through a crack in a door to glitch into a locked room in another player's base, or abusing chorus fruit to teleport through the server's world border.
* **5.5 Flight & Perk Restrictions (Public Warps):** The use of /fly, flight perks, or other unfair movement advantages is strictly prohibited in all public server /warps (e.g., Spawn, Crates, Shops, PvP arenas). These areas are designated as equal-footing zones for all players to prevent unfair advantages and visual clutter.  
  **Example:** Hovering out of reach above the boundary in /warp pvp to taunt players, or flying directly over the chests at /warp crates and blocking the view of players trying to open them.
* **5.6 Server Seed & External Mapping:** To encourage genuine exploration and maintain fair gameplay, the server seed is kept strictly confidential. Staff will not provide the seed under any circumstances, and attempting to crack or reverse-engineer the seed is prohibited.  
  **Example:** Opening a support ticket to ask for the server seed so you can use a website like Chunkbase to instantly find a Stronghold or slime chunk.
* **5.7 Permitted Modifications (Use at Your Own Risk):** Players are responsible for any mod they choose to install. Generally, purely cosmetic mods, performance-enhancing mods (e.g., OptiFine, Sodium, Shaders), and minimaps are permitted. If you are unsure if a mod is allowed, you must ask a staff member before using it.  
  **Example:** Using a minimap mod to set a waypoint for your house is allowed. However, turning on a "player radar" feature to track nearby enemies through walls is a bannable offense.

## **Article VI: Staff, Enforcement, & Community Guidelines**

Goal: Ensure smooth moderation and a drama-free environment.

* **6.1 Staff Respect:** Harassment, insults, or arguing with staff members regarding moderation decisions will not be tolerated.  
  **Example:** Calling a moderator names because they muted you for spamming.
* **6.2 No Mini-Modding:** While helping others is encouraged, do not attempt to act as staff or enforce rules yourself. Leave moderation to the official team.  
  **Example:** Threatening to ban a player or commanding them to "stop right now or else" instead of just opening a ticket.
* **6.3 External Drama:** Conflicts, beef, or drama originating from other servers, games, or social media must remain outside of Floreon.  
  **Example:** Continuing an argument in our Discord that started on a completely different Minecraft server.
* **6.4 Reporting Guidelines:** If you witness a rule violation, experience harassment, or find a bug, report it to staff via a ticket immediately. Do not engage the offender.  
  **Example:** Quietly opening a ticket and providing a screenshot of a player hacking, rather than yelling "HACKER!" in global chat.

## **Article VII: The "Catch-All" Clause**

**The Spirit of the Rules:** These rules cannot cover every possible scenario. The staff team reserves the right to take disciplinary action against any behavior that violates the intent of these rules, harms the community, or disrupts the peaceful environment of Floreon, even if that specific action is not explicitly listed here. "Loophole abuse" or rule-lawyering will result in strict punishment. Think before you click.

## **Final Note**

Thank you for taking the time to read our guidelines. Ultimately, these rules exist to protect your hard work, your gameplay, and the community as a whole. Play fair, respect your fellow players, and enjoy your adventure on Floreon!
