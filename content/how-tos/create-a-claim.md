To claim land, you'll need a **Golden Hoe**. If you ever want to check who owns a specific spot, just right-click the area with a **stick**. You'll also get more claim blocks automatically just by playing and staying online!

## How do I add someone to a claim?

To give a friend permission in your claim, type `/flan menu`, click "**Edit permission group**," and left-click the paper that says "**Trusted**." After that, click the Anvil and type in their player name.

> [!WARNING]
> This is case-sensitive so make sure the username is correct.

## I don't see Trusted in my Claim-Groups

![How to create a Claim?: screenshot 2](/images/create-a-claim-2.webp)

If you only see a "**Visitor**" group in your menu, it just means your claim was made before **September 12th**, back before we updated the default settings. It's an easy fix, though:

* Click the Anvil in the Claim-Groups menu to create a new group.
* Right-click your new group to pick the permissions you want to give out.
* Go back to Claim-Groups, then left-click your new group and add the player's name there.
