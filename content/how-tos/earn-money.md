description: The three ways to earn CobbleDollars on Floreon.

---
There are only three ways to earn money on Floreon:

## 1. Sell items with /sellall

Use `/sellall` to sell the items you don't need straight from your inventory.

## 2. Defeat League trainers for the first time

Every gym leader and other trainer in the [Pokémon League](../pokemon-league/index.md) pays CobbleDollars the **first time** you beat them. The later trainers pay much more. Rematches don't pay again. See the [Regions](../pokemon-league/regions/index.md) pages for the amount each trainer gives.

## 3. Play at the Casino

Go to the Casino with `/warp Casino`. You can earn money by betting at the **slot machines** or at the **Blackjack tables**.

> [!WARNING] Bet carefully
> Betting can lose money as well as win it. Only bet what you can afford to lose.

## Spending and sharing money

Once you have money you can sell items to other players with a [Chest Shop](create-chest-shops.md) or list them on the [GTS](../features/gts.md). Send money to a friend with `/cobbledollars pay <player> <amount>`.
