Ultra Beasts come through the **Ultra Wormhole**. Travel there with the warp command `/warp UltraWormhole`. It is in the **End**.

## What you need

- **1 Wormhole Shard**
- No weather or time is needed. You can do it at any time.

Stand at the gate in the middle of the build and right-click it. If you don't have a Wormhole Shard, the gate tells you. When everything is right, the gate glows for a few seconds and then **one Ultra Beast comes through at random**.

## Getting a Wormhole Shard

- Beat **Molayne** in the Alola League. He gives a Wormhole Shard the first time you win.
- Buy it in the **BP Shop** at the Battle Tower for **100 BP**.

## Which Ultra Beast comes through?

Any one of these ten, picked at random each time:

- Nihilego
- Buzzwole
- Pheromosa
- Xurkitree
- Celesteela
- Kartana
- Poipole
- Stakataka
- Blacephalon
- **Guzzlord**, which is much rarer than the others

You can't choose which one. To get a specific one, you have to try again.

> [!TIP] Naganadel
> Naganadel can't come through the wormhole. Catch a **Poipole**, teach it **Dragon Pulse**, and level it up to evolve it into Naganadel.

## Good to know

- The Ultra Beast appears at **level 70**, with a 2% chance of being shiny.
- For **300 seconds** (5 minutes) only you can battle it or catch it. Other players get a message if they try.
- If nobody beats it, it disappears after **10 minutes**.
- After you use the wormhole you must wait **24 hours** before you can use it again.
- The Wormhole Shard is used up each time.
