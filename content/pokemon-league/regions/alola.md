> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Kukui completes the Alola League and gives you the Alola Champion tag. It also unlocks the Galar warp and starts the Galar League, with a fresh level cap.
