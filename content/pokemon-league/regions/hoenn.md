> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Steven Stone completes the Hoenn League and gives you the Hoenn Champion tag. It also unlocks the Sinnoh warp and starts the Sinnoh League, with a fresh level cap.
