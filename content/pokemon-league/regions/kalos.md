> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Diantha completes the Kalos League and gives you the Kalos Champion tag. It also unlocks the Alola warp and starts the Alola League, with a fresh level cap.
