> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Alder completes the Unova League and gives you the Unova Champion tag. It also unlocks the Kalos warp and starts the Kalos League, with a fresh level cap.
