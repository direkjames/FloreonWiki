> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Cynthia completes the Sinnoh League and gives you the Sinnoh Champion tag. It also unlocks the Unova warp and starts the Unova League, with a fresh level cap.
