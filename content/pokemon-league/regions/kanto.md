> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Blue completes the Kanto League and gives you the Kanto Champion tag. It also unlocks the Johto warp and starts the Johto League, with a fresh level cap.
