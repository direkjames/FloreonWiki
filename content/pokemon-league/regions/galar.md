The Galar League has 9 trainers, from level 20 to level 65. Beat them in order: each trainer needs the ones listed before they open up.

| Trainer | Role | Type | Level | Needs first | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Milo | Gym leader | Grass | 20 | Nothing | 2,000 CobbleDollars, Grass Badge, Miracle Seed, 3 Max Potion |
| Nessa | Gym leader | Water | 24 | Milo | 2,000 CobbleDollars, Water Badge, Mystic Water, 3 Max Potion |
| Kabu | Gym leader | Fire | 27 | Nessa | 3,500 CobbleDollars, Fire Badge, Charcoal Stick, 3 Max Potion |
| Bea | Gym leader | Fighting | 30 | Kabu | 3,500 CobbleDollars, Fighting Badge, Black Belt, 3 Max Potion |
| Opal | Gym leader | Fairy | 36 | Bea | 5,500 CobbleDollars, Fairy Badge Galar, Silk Scarf, 3 Max Potion |
| Gordie | Gym leader | Rock | 38 | Opal | 5,500 CobbleDollars, Rock Badge, Hard Stone, 3 Max Potion |
| Piers | Gym leader | Dark | 44 | Gordie | 8,000 CobbleDollars, Dark Badge, Black Glasses, 3 Max Potion |
| Raihan | Gym leader | Dragon / Ground | 48 | Piers | 8,000 CobbleDollars, Dragon Badge, Dragon Fang, 3 Max Potion |
| Leon | Champion | Mixed | 65 | Raihan | 22,500 CobbleDollars, 30 Rare Candy, Ability Capsule, Lucky Egg |

> [!NOTE] Rewards come once
> Every reward is given once per player, the first time you win. Rematches give nothing.

## After the Champion

Beating Leon completes the Galar League and gives you the Galar Champion tag. It also unlocks the Paldea warp and starts the Paldea League, with a fresh level cap.
