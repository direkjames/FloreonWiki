In the main games each region has its own battle gimmick. The League follows that order, so you earn each gimmick as you travel.

| Gimmick | Unlocks when you reach |
|---|---|
| Mega Evolution | Hoenn League |
| Z-Moves | Alola League |
| Dynamax | Galar League |
| Terastallization | Paldea League |

## Good to know

- The lock only applies to battles against League trainers. Wild battles and battles with other players are not affected.
- A locked gimmick button is hidden or does nothing in a League fight. The League screen (`L`) shows green for gimmicks you can use and red for locked ones.
- Trainers can use gimmicks too, following the main games. Expect a Mega Evolution from some Kalos trainers, Z-Moves in Alola, and so on.
- Reaching a region means starting its League after beating the Champion of the region before it.

> [!NOTE] Settings can change
> These regions are the current server settings. If staff change them, the League screen always shows what applies to you.
