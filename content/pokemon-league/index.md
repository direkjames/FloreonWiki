The Floreon Pokémon League takes you through nine regions: Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea. Every region has its gym leaders, an Elite Four and a Champion (Galar has no Elite Four). Levels follow the main games, so the later regions are a real challenge.

## How it works

:::steps
1. Start with the first gym leader of Kanto. Each trainer opens up only after you beat the one before.
2. Beat all gym leaders, then the Elite Four, then the Champion of the region.
3. Beating the Champion gives you a Champion tag, unlocks the warp to the next region and starts the next League.
4. Keep going until you finish Paldea.
:::

## Level cap

Your Pokémon have a level cap in the League. It rises as you beat trainers and starts again at the first gym of every new region, so each region begins with a gentle first fight.

## Rewards

Every trainer gives a reward the first time you win: CobbleDollars, a gym badge and useful items, and the Elite Four and Champions give more. Rematches are fine for practice but give nothing.

## The progress screen

Press `L` in game to open the League screen. You can change the key in your Controls settings. It shows:

- every region and trainer, with their skin, level and type
- what each trainer needs first and what they reward
- which trainers you have beaten
- your level cap, and which battle gimmicks you can use

Use the search box to find a trainer in any region.
